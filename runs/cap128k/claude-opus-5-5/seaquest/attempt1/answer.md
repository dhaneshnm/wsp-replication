# Hand-written Seaquest policy: design and weights

## Strategy

The submarine stays parked in columns 5–6. It never steers horizontally except to **turn**. Pressing the opposite direction flips the facing, and the two occupied cells stay the same.

- **Vertical tracking.** The sub moves up or down toward rows that hold targets, then holds that row.
  - Divers count as worth 2, enemies 1, and enemy bullets −1.
  - Nearer rows count more.
- **Holding a row.** It holds while an enemy or diver is in its own row:
  - It turns to face the enemy and fires.
  - Divers come to it.
- **Dodging.** If an enemy bullet is in its own row, it leaves the row.
- **Not moving into danger.** It will not step into an adjacent row when something dangerous sits near its column there.
- **Resting depth.** With no targets, it drifts to rows 4–5.
- **Row 1 is forbidden.** Rows 0–1 are only passed through when diving or surfacing. Pressing up at row 0 or 1 is strongly blocked unless the sub is surfacing, because surfacing with no diver ends the episode.
- **Surfacing.** It goes up when the oxygen bar's column 2 is off (oxygen below 60) and the diver bar's column 8 is on (at least one diver).
- **Default action** is fire.

## Hidden layer 1 (32 units; units 27–31 unused)

The per-row units are gated: each ch0 cell in row k has weight 15 and the bias is −30. So a unit is ≈ −1 unless the sub is in row k, where it outputs tanh(signal). Layer 2 then sees exactly one active value plus constants.

| Units | Name | What it computes (for the sub's row k) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | V_k | Signed vertical urge (+ = down). Sums distance-weighted target values from other rows (rows 0–1 excluded), repulsion from danger near the sub's column in adjacent rows, and a depth bias. At rows 0–2 it is strongly biased downward. |
| 9–15 | T_k (k = 2..8) | Turn signal: (enemy count in cols 7–9 − in cols 0–3) + 2 × facing. Facing (+0.5 = left) is read from the back cell (ch4) relative to ch0 within cols 4–7. |
| 16–22 | E_k (k = 2..8) | Own-row status: tanh(3·(2·reward − 1.5·danger)) in the own row, plus a hold term for danger near the column in adjacent rows. Positive = hold; negative (a bullet) = dodge. |
| 23 | R01 | Indicator that the sub is in row 0 or 1. |
| 24 | SURF | Oxygen low and at least one diver aboard. |
| 25 | XR | Sub has drifted too far right (from the column-weighted sum of ch0). |
| 26 | XL | Sub has drifted too far left. |

## Layer 2

| Unit | Name | What it computes |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | DOWN | Down when v > 0.3 unless holding; in dodge mode, down when v > −0.7. Suppressed by SURF. |
| 1 | UP | The mirror of DOWN, plus a strong SURF term, minus a strong block at rows 0–1. |
| 2 | TURN R | High-gain threshold on T. |
| 3 | TURN L | High-gain threshold on T, blocked at rows 0–1. |
| 4–5 | Centering | Steer back toward columns 5–6, only while not holding a row. |

## Output

- Each layer-2 unit adds about ±12 to its action's logit; centering adds 8.
- Fire has logit 0 and no-op has −15, so fire wins whenever nothing else is active.
